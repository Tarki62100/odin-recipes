Hard Boiled Eggs Recipe

Credit:Yuliya Galagan

Ingredients:

  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • ¼ cup distilled white vinegar
  • 6 cups water
  • 8 large eggs

Directions:

  1. Combine salt, vinegar, and water in a large pot, and bring to a boil over high heat.

  2. Add eggs one at a time, being careful not to crack them.

    Reduce the heat to a gentle boil, and cook for 14 minutes.

  3. Once eggs have cooked, remove them from the hot water, and place into a container of ice water or cold, running water.

    Cool completely, about 15 minutes.

    Store in the refrigerator up to 1 week.
