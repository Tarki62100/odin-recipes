Manti Recipe

Credit:Erva Nur

Descripton:

Manti is a historical turkish dish that has been eaten by sultans and commoners alike,
which is why it still lies in many turkish people's hearts today!
The savoury lick of this dish, when combined with garlic yogurt and sauce, is impeccable,
and the texture of the thin dough will make it melt in your mouth.
In this recipe, we will learn how to make this wonderful dish!

Ingredients:

Dough:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • ½ teaspoon water, or as needed

Filling:

  • 2 onions peeled
  • ½ pound ground beef
  • salt and pepper to taste

Red pepper oil sauce:

  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon red pepper flakes

Garlic yogurt:

  • 1 (8 ounce) container plain yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
